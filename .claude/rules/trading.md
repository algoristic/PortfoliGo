# Trading: current state

What exists in the code, one bullet per AP. Update it when an AP changes this area (projektplan §2.4).

- Order rules (AP 5.1a, TS §7.5): framework-free `trading/domain`. Models in `models/order.model.ts` (`ORDER_SIDES`, `ORDER_TYPES` `market`/`limit`/`stop`/`stop-limit`, validity `day`/`date`/`gtc`, `ORDER_STATUSES` with the active `open`/`triggered`, size as `quantity` in millionths or `amount` in cents (buy only), `OrderSpec`, `OrderChange`, `Order` with `placedAt`, `expiresAt`, `reserved`, `triggeredAt`, `execution`, `closedAt`, `rejection`; codes `ORDER_COMMAND_ERRORS`, part of `ENGINE_ERRORS`, and at execution `ORDER_REJECTIONS`; `ORDER_HISTORY_LENGTH` 500) and `models/position.model.ts` (`Position`: depot, instrument, quantity; lots since AP 5.2). Rules: `order-validity-logic.ts` (`orderExpiry`: „day“ ends with the next close of the instrument's exchange, `isOrderExpired`, `validityError`, at most a year), `order-execution-logic.ts` (`orderDecision` per tick at bid and ask, `midRulesOutExecution` as a cheap pre-check, `amountOrderQuantity`, `orderFill` with fees, `executionError`, `executionTransaction`: exchange fee booked as a bank fee), `order-reservation-logic.ts` (`orderReservation` with `RESERVATION_BUFFER` 5 %, `reservedAmount`, `freeQuantity`), `order-command-logic.ts` (`orderPlacementError`, `orderChangeError`, `changedReservation`, `isAtPriceFloor`, shape guards `isOrderSpec`/`isOrderChange`, also used by the protocol guards), `position-logic.ts` (`holdingOf`, `hasPositions`; since AP 5.2 the lot bookings below). Example tables in TS §7.5; all rule files are in the mutation tests. `trading/feature/orders` is still a placeholder page.
- Orders in the engine (AP 5.1b, TS §7.5): `OrderSystem` (`engine/domain/order-system.ts`) on `PlayerState.orders` (active, placement order), `orderHistory` (closed, oldest first, ≤ 500), `orderSequence` (`ord-<n>`) and `positions`. Game commands `placeOrder` (result `orderId`), `changeOrder`, `cancelOrder`; checks every active order in ticks with an open exchange (`needsTick`, `runUntil` processes those ticks only), checks a new or changed order at once when its exchange is open (market orders execute during the pause), expires orders by the task `order-expiry`, books executions through the banking system and the units on the positions. Events `order-triggered`, `order-executed`, `order-rejected`, `order-expired`; notifications in `notification-system.ts`. Reservations lower `available` of clearing accounts (`availableAfterReservations`); depots with holdings or active orders cannot close. `drainChangedOrders()`/`drainChangedDepots()` for the host. Scenarios `engine/domain/game-engine-orders.spec.ts` (test banks of TS §7.5, `TestMarket`), properties in `engine-properties.spec.ts`, budgets in `engine.perf.spec.ts` (a year in batch mode with 20 share orders ~0,9 s, with 10 ETF and 10 share orders ~2,8 s; budget 5 s).
- Protocol and projection (AP 5.1c, TS §8.2–§8.4): the three commands through the host (write-ahead, reply `{ tick, orderId }` for `placeOrder`, engine codes passed through); `projection.trading` (`TradingProjection` in `engine/domain/models/trading-view.model.ts`: `OrderView`, `DepotHoldingsView` with `positions` of `{ instrumentId, quantity, free }`), built by `tradingProjection` (`engine/domain/order-projection.ts`): full after `loadPlayer` (active orders, then the history; all depots), else the changed orders and depots plus the depot of every changed order. `AccountView.reserved`. `EngineService.placeOrder(spec)`, `changeOrder(orderId, change)`, `cancelOrder(orderId)`, `trading$` (`TradingProjectionUpdate`: `tick`, `full`, `orders`, `depots`; replace by ID, trim the history to `ORDER_HISTORY_LENGTH` in the UI, a depot missing from a full projection's later updates is empty). Host tests `engine/data-access/worker/engine-host-orders.spec.ts`. No trading slice yet (AP 5.4).
- Positions, lots, payouts, valuation (AP 5.2, TS §6.5, §7.4, §7.6, §7.9): `portfolio/domain` holds `models/lot.model.ts` (`Lot` `{ buyTick, quantity, cost }`, `LotSale`), `models/distribution.model.ts` (`DISTRIBUTION_KINDS` `dividend`/`fund-distribution`, `PendingDistribution`), `lot-logic.ts` (`lotCostShare` exact with `BigInt`, `sellLots` FIFO, `lotTotals`, `averageCost` in euros), `distribution-logic.ts` (`distributionAmount`, `distributionPaymentTick` = 06:00 of the payment date, `pendingDistributionsFor`) and `valuation-logic.ts` (`holdingsValue`: per holding `marketValue(quantity, bid)`). `Position` has `lots`, `realizedGain`, `distributions` (earned at ex-dates); `position-logic.ts`: `findPosition`, `bookPurchase` (lot cost = debit with costs), `saleOf`/`bookSale` (FIFO, realized = credit after costs − cost basis; an empty position is removed), `addDistribution`, `copyPosition`. `executionTransaction(…, sale?)` adds `costBasis`/`realizedGain` to sale params; the journal keeps a sold-out position's history (`refs.instrumentId`). Engine: `OrderSystem` books lots and calls the tax hook `taxCapitalIncome` after a sale; `DistributionSystem` (`engine/domain/distribution-system.ts`) records entitlements at the ex-date's open (`exDate`, before the tick's orders), pays them in the task `distribution-payment` (rank before `bank-run`) against `external:issuer:<instrument>`, then the tax hook and the event `distribution-paid`; `redirect`/`pendingFor` for closing accounts. `Valuation` (`engine/domain/valuation.ts`, bid of `quoteAt(…, 0)`), `GameEngine.securitiesValue()`. `PositionView` adds `cost`, `lots`, `realizedGain`, `distributions`; values come from quotes in the UI. Scenarios `engine/domain/game-engine-portfolio.spec.ts` (ex-dates found from the mini world's events), properties (lots ↔ journal) in `engine-properties.spec.ts`, budget case with 30 positions in `engine.perf.spec.ts` (~0,2 s).
- Taxes on sales (AP 5.3, TS §7.7): after `bookSale` the `OrderSystem` books the tax on `realizedGain` through `taxCapitalIncome` (refs `orderId`, `depotId`, `instrumentId`); `order-executed` carries `tax` (credit after costs and taxes = `total − tax`, US-HD-02). The sale preview for the order ticket (AP 5.4) is `saleTaxPreview` in `taxes/domain` / `TaxesFacade.saleTaxPreview$(lots, quantity, proceeds)` with `proceeds` = credit after costs from `orderFill`.
