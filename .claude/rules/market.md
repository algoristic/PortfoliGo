# Market: current state

What exists in the code, by topic. When an AP changes this area, work the change into the matching topic instead of adding a bullet per AP (projektplan §2.4).

## Market model (`market/domain`; AP 1.4–1.6, 1.9; E-20, E-30–E-33)

- **Day step:** `createMarketContext(seedKey, world, config = DEFAULT_MARKET_CONFIG)` (typed-array layout), `createMarketState`, `stepMarketDay`/`advanceMarket` (support points of a trading day into a reused `MarketDay`; weekends skipped), `runPrehistory` (epoch → game start), `snapshotMarket`/`restoreMarket`, `marketChecksum`, `priceFromLog` (price floor). Day streams use `StreamFamily` (`simulation/util`, bit-identical to `createStream`, much faster).
- **History:** `FactorHistory` holds month-start states incl. `U_i` and the dividend/index state, so `recomputeFactorDays`/`recomputeInstrumentHistory`/`recomputeSupportDays` rebuild any past day bit for bit. `marketStateAt(context, history, day)` (`market-history.ts`) runs from the month start before `day` up to it (without its own step), bit-identical with the uninterrupted run; used by `GameEngine.marketAtDayStart()` (export) and the import's `MarketHistoryRun` (AP 6.1, TS §10.2).
- **Idiosyncratic part (AP 7.3d, E-22 new, TS §6.4):** `U_i` = permanent random walk (full compensator `−½·((1 − w)·σ_d² + v_J)` per day, decaying alpha, zero-beta drift `(1 − β)·r_z`, jumps, dividend markdowns) + transient part `T_i` (`MarketState.idioTransient`, share `w` = `idioTransientShare` of the diffusion variance, returns with `idioReversion` to `τ(t) = −½·v_T·g(t)`, `transientTargetAt`). No extra draw: `T_i` takes the fixed combination `a·ε_on + b·ε` of the two daily shocks (`transientShockWeights`, orthogonal split, `MarketContext.transientWeights`); `U_i` keeps its shocks and draw order. An extra normal per instrument-day broke the import budget (TS §14.3). Variances per instrument in the context (`permanentVariance`, `transientVariance`, `jumpVariance` from `idioVariances`). Non-world factors still return to `factorPathAt` (θ_k checked in AP 7.3d and weighed again in AP 7.3e: kept at 7 years, the trade-off premiums vs. R12 is the product owner's). `expectedLogPath(alpha, alphaWeight, drift, years, variance, varianceWeight)` serves both.
- **Volatility clustering (AP 7.3d, 7.1b-02):** the diffusion of a day scales with `idioDayScale(context, day.factorVols[WORLD_FACTOR])` = `√((s/s̄)^{2φ} / M)` (`idioVolCoupling` φ; `M` = `idioVolNormalization` from `stationaryPhaseShares` and `idioVolNormalization` in `idio-volatility-logic.ts`, computed from the config). The session bridge (`day-curve.ts`) and `ReferenceSimulation` use the same scale; a fresh `MarketDay` has world vol 0 → scale 0 (tests set `factorVols` or `idioVolCoupling: 0`).
- **Activity weighting:** factor intervals are weighted by exchange activity (`activityIntervalYears(…, regionId)`, weights from `MarketConfig`, never from the companies); region factors have their own profile (`regionClosedActivity`); `MarketContext.intervalYears`/`sqrtIntervalYears` are per factor at index `k · intervals + j`. The spread factor follows the world factor volatility (`spreadVolFactor`).
- **Dividends:** every company has a frequency and rhythm (`dividend.schedule`, stream `world/dividends`); non-payers start at `dividend.startDay` (`dividendInitiationYears`); target yields move to `dividendLifecycle.longRunYield` (`dividendDragAt`, `yieldFromDrag`; `dividendYieldAt` in `market-context.ts`); since AP 7.3d no return recovers the markdowns, so no target path holds them. `dividend-logic.ts`: ex-dates, announcement 20 market days ahead, payment date, amount per share in cents with two decimals (euros with four, AP 7.3d; the credit rounds to cents in `distributionAmount`), markdown. `MarketState` holds `logClose`, `nextExDay`, `dividendCents`, `indexReinvest`; `stepInstrument` writes into it; `MarketDay` reports announcements, ex-dates and `R` per boundary.
- **Indices:** `IndexLayout` in `MarketContext.indices` (constituents, capping fixed at the epoch, constant divisor, index session); `stepIndices` advances `R` from the previous closes.
- **ETFs:** `startPrice`, distributing ETFs a `schedule` (stream `world/etf-terms`); `EtfLayout` in `MarketContext.etfs`; `stepEtfs` (after `stepIndices`) advances `k` (TER, tracking noise from `market/etfs/<day>`) and the distribution pot per boundary; NAV from the indicative values of the constituents.
- **Series order** (`series.ts`): shares, price indices, total return indices, ETFs (`etfSeries`) share one index space for `PriceSource`, `DayCurves.seriesCurve`, `dailyCandlesFromSupport` and `createHighLowFill`.

## Prices, candles and quotes (AP 1.5, 1.6b, 6.4; TS §6.8)

- `DayCurves` builds the minute bridges of one day (`bridge-logic.ts`, streams `…/<day>/bridge[/<interval>]` via `StreamFamily.create(n, suffix)`). Bridges fill minute by minute, so `fillBridge(…, last)` computes only the needed start of a session (ETF curves: members up to the ETF close, none outside the ETF session), bit-equal to the whole curve.
- **Pitfall:** keep imported constants/functions out of the innermost minute loops (`factorAt`, `loadingsOf` as locals) — under Vitest every import access is a getter call.
- `PriceSource` is the engine's price source (`priceAt`, `minuteCurve`, `candles` `1m`–`1w`, `rememberDay`, LRU cache, `indicativeAt`, `quoteAt(series, tick, now, venueMarkup)`). Every query takes `now` and never returns values after it.
- `DayCurves.indicativeCurve` (frozen `U_i`, factor bridges); `SupportValues` (opens/closes of all series from support points); `quote-logic.ts` (`marketSpread`, `quote`: bid/ask on the price grid); `candle-logic.ts` (candle convention); `createHighLowFill` (time-sliced background high/low job).
- `reference-simulation.ts`: independent minute-by-minute reference (follows `intradayVolProfile`); the lab runs it in the worker, the calibration suite compares against it.
- Consolidation of old years: `candle-consolidation-logic.ts` (`DAILY_CANDLE_YEARS`, `newestConsolidatedYear`, `weekParts`) is the rule the worker applies (see `engine.md`); daily blocks reaching more than 20 game years back get one candle per week part (at its first market day); chart code is unaware.

## Calibration (AP 1.9c, E-34; TS §6.9)

- `DEFAULT_MARKET_CONFIG` and `DEFAULT_WORLD_CONFIG` were calibrated in AP 1.9c and recalibrated in AP 7.3e on `broad-1` … `broad-20` (phases as a business cycle with close trend targets; world trend +1 point, world vol targets ×1.06, region 8.5 %, zero-beta return 6 %; smaller idio vol and jump spread of mid and small caps; `riskClassFactorVols` mirror the factor vol targets, risk class bounds are quintiles of the full volatility over `idio-1` … `idio-20`).
- `lab/domain/calibration-targets.calibration.spec.ts` checks every target band and the long-term limits (checkpoints 25, 50, 100 years; per-checkpoint bounds via `LongTermLimit.atYears` and `limitAt`) on the median of the 20 fresh seeds `TEST_SEEDS.calibrationBroad` via `LabSimulation` (E-34 extended, AP 7.3e: targets with a minimum period up to ten years over the prehistory **and** over the long run; return and bear markets over 110 years) and prints a report of all rows (`lab/domain/testing/calibration-run.ts`: `measureCalibrationRun`, `calibrationReport`). It takes about 5.5 min (20 seeds one after another); `calib-1` … `calib-5` remain the seeds of the lab's seed comparison and of the other calibration specs. Correlation targets are measured over large caps (all pairs as info rows); emerging-markets overnight share is info only.
- Info rows without band (AP 7.3a, 7.3d, TS §6.9): weekly/monthly world volatility, autocorrelations (incl. `stock-abs-autocorrelation-lag1`, ACF(|r|, 1) median of the shares), drawdown measures, regional indices, common bear-market count, highest price, share of negative 30-year returns. The premiums (`premiumMeasures`: equal weight ≤ 3 points, 20 deepest distances to the high ≤ 5 points, from 5 years, prehistory and long run) have bands since AP 7.3e (product owner). The info run `calibration-seeds.calibration.spec.ts` (AP 7.3a) became the check in AP 7.3e and is gone.
- Overnight shares of Europe and North America (34.5 / 33.6 % at 35 %), large-cap volatility, the correlation between sectors and the bear markets sit close to their bounds (weaker region factors push the overnight shares up, TS §6.9): run `npm run test:calibration` on every model/world change; recalibrate instead of widening bands (product-owner decisions).
- Other calibration specs: `market.calibration.spec.ts`, `idio-path.calibration.spec.ts`, `factor-level.calibration.spec.ts`, `bridge-equivalence.calibration.spec.ts`. Golden master in `golden-master.ts` (`GOLDEN_CHECKSUMS` of `golden-1` plus `worlds` over `worlds-1` … `worlds-20`; `GOLDEN_WORLDS_CHECKSUM` over 200 seeds in the logic test only; refrozen with `SIM_VERSION` 2 in AP 7.3d), properties in `market-properties.spec.ts`, perf in `market.perf.spec.ts`.
- **Long-term limits (E-22, R12; product owner after AP 7.3e):** top five ≤ 45 % after 25 years and ≤ 65 % after 50 and 100 years (`topFiveShare.atYears`), below 1 € ≤ 10 %, at the floor ≤ 2 %; ≤ 40 % for the top five only with the rebalanced indices of M9 (E-40).
- **Open targets (E-39):** `CalibrationTarget.openUntil`/`LongTermLimit.openUntil`: the suite reports their median instead of checking it. None is open since AP 7.3e; model APs of stage 2 use the mark until their milestone's calibration step.

## Market slice (`market/feature/state`; AP 4.1)

- `provideMarketState()` (in `provideGameState()`, includes `provideLocalStorageService()`). Instruments and quotes as entities (quotes by `series`), movers; instruments load once after `loadPlayerSuccess`.
- **Quote subscriptions** by key: views call `MarketFacade.watchQuotes` etc. → `quotesSubscriptionChanged`/`quotesSubscriptionEnded`, `moversSubscriptionStarted`/`moversSubscriptionEnded`. `watchQuotes$` keeps one `EngineService.quotes` subscription per key from the `loadPlayer` reply on (new set before ending the old), ends it with the key and on `leaveGame`; packages of all keys within `QUOTES_BUNDLE_WINDOW_MS` (100 ms, a pending task) become one `receivedQuotes`.
- Guards in `market.routes.ts`: `enterMarketsPage`/`leaveMarketsPage` (movers, price indices without sector indices), `enterInstrumentPage`/`leaveInstrumentPage` (route param `instrumentId`), watchlist guards below.

## Instrument chart (AP 4.2, 4.3, 5.5b, 5.8; US-CH-01/02, US-PF-06; TS §11.7)

- **View logic** `chart-view-logic.ts`: `CHART_RANGE_INTERVALS` (1T→1m, 5T→15m, 1M→1h, 6M/1J→1d, 5J/Max→1w), `CHART_RANGE_MARKET_DAYS` (23 per month, `max` unbounded), `chartStartRequest`/`olderChartRequest` (optional `interval`), `trimChartBlock`, `prependCandles`, 1T day navigation (`shownChartDay`/`previousChartDay`/`nextChartDay`/`chosenChartDay`), live rule `liveCandleStep`/`applyLiveCandle`.
- **Resolution:** `isChartResolutionAllowed` (more than one candle, ≤ `MAX_CANDLE_LIMIT` counted with 1,440 minutes a day, intraday ≤ `MAX_INTRADAY_DAYS`; Max allows none → only „Auto“), `chartResolutions`, `keptChartResolution`, `chartInterval`. `market.chart.resolution` (`null` = „Auto“) is never stored, reset by `loadedChartSettings`, kept on a new range while allowed; `clickedChartResolution` is ignored when not allowed or unchanged.
- **Slice part** `market.chart` (`InstrumentChartState`: candles of the shown chart only, chosen day or `null` = live, `generation`, `hasOlder`, loading flags). Effects `loadChart$` (after enter, new range/day, `detectedChartGap`; once instrument and clock are known), `loadOlderChartCandles$` (`scrolledToChartStart`), `watchChart$` (`chartCandles` while the block reaches now → `receivedLiveCandle`), `showChartFailure$`. `selectChartView` depends on the game day only; it and `selectChartSubscription` use `chartInterval`.
- **Indicators:** `chart-indicator-logic.ts` (`candleTailChange`, `ChartIndicatorSet`: continue the `analysis/domain` trackers when only the newest candle changed or one was appended, else recompute). `market.chart.indicators` (`ChartIndicator`, `market/ui/models/chart-indicator.model.ts`; `id` `i<n>`, ≤ `MAX_CHART_INDICATORS` 6); `submittedChartIndicator` (`id` `null` = add); `withChartIndicator`; `nextIndicatorColor` = first unused colour, else `chart-1` (colours may repeat). `instrument-chart` computes the overlays (Bollinger dashed, upper band `fillTo` lower band) and keeps the edit state.
- **Settings** `pgo.ui.chart` (`CHART_SETTINGS_VERSION` 1: range, type, indicators; TS §9.3): read by `loadChartSettings$` on `enterGame` (`parseChartSettings` in `market.utils.ts`; an indicator without valid colour gets the first colour the earlier ones leave free), written by `saveChartSettings$` after every change (toast on failure).
- **Trade markers:** `chart-marker-logic.ts` (`ChartTrade`, `candleEnd`, `tradeMarkers`: trade on the candle whose `[start, end)` holds its tick, others dropped). `instrument-chart` input `trades`, labelled „K“/„V“; only the position detail passes trades.
- **UI:** `market/feature/instrument-chart` (smart), `market/ui/chart-toolbar` (select `market.instrument.chart.resolution`, disallowed options `disabled`), `candle-readout`, `chart-indicators` (legend with crosshair/newest values), `form-indicator` (Signal Forms, emits `SubmittedIndicator` `{ spec, color }`, keeps the colour on kind change; component test), `labels/indicator-labels.ts` + pipe `indicatorLabel`.

## Instrument page (AP 4.5; US-MK-03/05/06)

- `market.instrumentDetail` (`InstrumentDetailState`, shown instrument only): enter/leave empty it and raise `generation` (switching instruments runs leave+enter); answers of older generations are dropped.
- `loadInstrumentDetail$` waits for master data, asks for details and in parallel figures, dividends (shares, distributing ETFs) and composition (indices, ETFs); these reload on a new market day (`selectMarketDay`) and when the instrument's session opens (`selectRouteInstrumentSession`: the quote's `previousClose`); an unknown ID only asks for details. `loadInstrumentDetailFailure` with `unknown-instrument` → `notFound` („Wertpapier nicht gefunden“), other errors toast (`showInstrumentDetailFailure$`).
- View rules `state/instrument-view.utils.ts` (`instrumentHeadView`: spread absolute and relative to the middle, price-grid decimals, exchange status — for indices of their region's exchanges / all exchanges; `instrumentDetailView`; `compositionSegments`: five segments + `other`). `selectInstrumentHead` follows the clock, `selectInstrumentDetailView` doesn't.
- UI (`market/ui`, models `models/instrument-view.model.ts`): `instrument-quote` (`appValueFlash` on last price, iNAV „Stand …“ for ETFs; watchlist star projected), `instrument-figures`, `instrument-profile` (per kind; index cap labelled „Höchstgewicht bei Auflage“, the cap is fixed at the epoch), `index-composition` (top 10, region/sector donuts), `dividend-overview` (empty states for accumulating ETFs and non-payers; amounts per share with up to four decimals, component test). Labels `labels/company-profile-text.ts` (`focusLabel` for all world focus codes, `companyProfileText` for the four templates), `labels/instrument-labels.ts`.
- „Kaufen“/„Verkaufen“ via `trading/ui/trade-actions` (see `trading.md`).
- Value flash: components keep `flashEnabled` inputs, but the device setting acts app-wide via `VALUE_FLASH_ENABLED` (see `shared.md`), so no page passes it. Gain/loss colours follow the token pair; charts re-read tokens on `<html>` class changes (AP 6.2).
