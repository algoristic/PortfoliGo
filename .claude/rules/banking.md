# Banking and taxes: current state

What exists in the code, one bullet per AP. Update it when an AP changes this area (projektplan §2.4).

- Home bank `findHomeBank` (`banking/domain`) and `PlayerState.homeBankId` (AP 2.4).
- Ledger and account model (AP 3.1, TS §7.2, §7.3): framework-free `banking/domain`. Account model (`models/account.model.ts`: `ACCOUNT_KINDS` `current`/`savings`/`clearing`, accounts and depots with bank, number, `openedAt`/`closedAt`; `accountId`, `depotId`, `accountNumber` in `account-logic.ts` from one counter per player, `acc-<n>`/`dep-<n>`, ten-digit numbers); bank conditions in `models/bank-conditions.model.ts`; ledger (`models/ledger.model.ts`: `TRANSACTION_TYPES`, external counter accounts; `ledger-logic.ts`: `bookTransaction(balances, seq, draft)` checks at least two postings, whole cents, sum 0 and known accounts, throws on violations and sets `balanceAfter` per posting; `journalBalances`; constants such as `EMPLOYER_ACCOUNT`, `bankFeesAccount(id)`); `fee-logic.ts` (`fee(model, volume)` for every `FeeModel`, `custodyFee`) and `interest-logic.ts` (act/365 daily interest unrounded, `overdraftDailyInterest(balance, limit, rates)`, `clearingAccountDailyInterest`, `bookedInterest` rounds when booking). The determinism lint rule also covers the `domain` folders of `banking`, `trading`, `portfolio` and `taxes`. `PlayerState` holds accounts, depots, `balances` (`Map`), `salaryAccountId`, `accountSequence` and `nextTransactionSeq`; `GameEngine.start` opens `acc-1` at the home bank as salary account and books the start capital as transaction 1 (`opening-balance` against `external:opening-balance`; none for 0). Every booking goes through `bookTransaction` into a buffer that the host collects with `GameEngine.drainTransactions()` and writes to the `transactions` store (batched within 1 s, `transactionWriteIntervalMs`; each snapshot writes the unconfirmed ones in the same IndexedDB transaction; `EngineStorage.saveTransactions`/`loadTransactions(player, account?)`, index `byAccount`). `SCHEMA_VERSION` 2: M2 players are outdated.
- Household and periodic bookings (AP 3.2, TS §3.8, §7.4, §7.7): pure rules in `banking/domain` (`bank-run-logic.ts`: `BANK_RUN_STEPS`, `bankRunPlan(tick)`, `nextBankRunTick`, `isInterestPayoutDue`, `monthOf`, `monthKey`; `living-costs-logic.ts`: `livingCosts(seedKey, base, month)` from the stream `life/living-costs/<YYYY-MM>`; `inflow-logic.ts`: `startInflows`, `openInflows`, `recordInflows`, `closeInflowMonth`; `account-fee-logic.ts`: `monthlyAccountFee`, `closingAccountFees`; `overdraft-logic.ts`: `overdraftLimits` per bank from the salary window, `availableAmount`, `accountEvents`; `monthly-values-logic.ts`: `addToMonthlyValues`, `savingsRate`) and the tax core `taxes/domain/tax-logic.ts` (`openTaxYear`, `assessTaxableIncome`, `closeTaxYear`, `taxTransactionType`). The engine's `BankingSystem` (`engine/domain/banking-system.ts`) works on `PlayerState` (`overdraftLimits`, `inflows`, `interest` as `InterestAccrual` with running and pending credit/debit, `taxYear`, `monthlyValues`, `closedMonths`): every booking goes through `book` (inflows, monthly values, `account-uncovered`/`overdraft-limit-exceeded` events with account, balance and limit); the day change accrues the previous day's interest on its end-of-day balance; the `bank-run` task at 06:00 (`nextBankRunTick`) runs the steps in order (limits, salary, credit interest each followed by its tax and an `interest-credited` event, living costs, account fees, overdraft and debit interest), books only the pending interest of the settled period and skips amounts of 0; `month-end` closes monthly values, inflow window and interest period, `year-end` the tax year. `openAccount`/`registerAccount` and `settleAccount(id, tick)` (interest up to the day before and fees up to the closing day) are ready for the bank commands of AP 3.3. Scenarios in `banking-system.spec.ts` use the test banks of `engine/domain/testing/banking-game.ts` (`startBankingGame`, `withTestBanks`, `withBooks` to add accounts, balances or another salary account to a snapshot). `SCHEMA_VERSION` 3.
- Bank commands and banking slice (AP 3.3, TS §7.4, §8.2–§8.4, §11.3): game commands `openAccount` (`current`, `savings`, `depot` with clearing account; reply `accountId`, `depotId`, `tick`), `closeAccount` (settlement and remainder to `targetAccountId`), `setSalaryAccount`, `transfer` (`TransferPayload`) with the codes `BANK_COMMAND_ERRORS` (`banking/domain/models/bank-command.model.ts`, part of `ENGINE_ERRORS`); engine rules in `engine/domain/bank-commands.ts`, pure checks in `banking/domain/bank-command-logic.ts` (`MAX_PURPOSE_LENGTH`). Queries `banks()`, `closingPreview(id)`, `monthlyValues()` on the `EngineService`; event `projection` (`projection$`: `tick`, `full`, changed `AccountView`s from `engine/domain/account-projection.ts`, `netWorth`; full after every `loadPlayer`, before every command reply, else ≤ 4/s); the host writes `netWorth` into `runtime`, so `PlayerListEntry.netWorth` comes from the `PlayersService`. Rejected game commands count as applied (replay never applies them later). Slice `banking` (`banking/feature/state`, `provideBankingState()` in `provideGameState()`; `BankingFacade`): accounts as entities (closed ones kept), banks (loaded after `loadPlayerSuccess`), `netWorth$`, pending commands per kind (`pending$`, `isPending$(command)`); `watchProjection$` takes the last full projection before the `loadPlayer` reply and every one after it; `enterGame`/`leaveGame` reset the slice. Selectors: `accounts$` (with `bank`, `null` until loaded), `openAccounts$`, `accountTotals$` (per kind and total of the open accounts), `salaryAccount$`, `account$(id)`, `targetAccounts$(sourceId)`, `accountsOfBank$(bankId)`. Commands `openAccount`, `closeAccount`, `setSalaryAccount`, `transfer` dispatch `submittedX`; effects answer with `xSuccess` (reply data, e.g. the new account ID) or `xFailure` and show toasts; `bankCommandFailureMessage` (`banking-errors.utils.ts`) maps every code to a German text. Net worth in the header (`shell.header.net-worth`, from `md`) and in the player list (`players.list.<id>.net-worth`); page tests read amounts with `parseEuros` (`src/testing/parse.ts`).
- Accounts and statement (AP 3.4, TS §7.2, §8.6, §11.3, §14.3): the worker query `statement` (`EngineService.statement`, newest first, `offset`/`limit` ≤ 200, `total`, search rules in TS §7.2). The banking slice holds the open statement page in `statement` (filter, `generation`, `total`, only the loaded window `offset`/`entries`, rendered range); the account comes from the router store (`selectStatementAccountId`). Guards `enterAccountStatementPage`/`leaveAccountStatementPage`; `loadStatement$` loads the first page once the session phase is `loaded`, again on filter changes (search debounced by `STATEMENT_SEARCH_DEBOUNCE_MS`), the window around the rendered rows (`statementWindowRequest` in `banking.utils.ts`: pages of 50) and on projections of the account; scrolling waits for a running query (the worker answers one after the other). Periods from the game time with `statementPeriodRange` (`banking/domain`). UI in `banking/ui`: `account-card`, `account-totals`, `account-header`, `statement-filter`, `statement-list` (CDK virtual scrolling, rows of 64 px, skeletons for rows not loaded; booking texts in `statement-text.utils.ts`, rows in `statement-list.utils.ts`), labels in `labels/banking-labels.ts` and the pipe `accountKindLabel`. Pages `banking/feature/accounts` and `account-statement` (unknown account → empty state); AP 3.5 puts its actions into `[accountActions]` of `app-account-header`. Page tests with virtual scrolling call `installScrollViewport(height)` (`src/testing/scroll-viewport.ts`) before rendering and scroll with `scrollTo(element, top)`; system test `e2e/banking.e2e.ts`. Main-thread budget per statement page in `statement-page.perf.spec.ts` (Node with `@angular/compiler` and `@angular/localize/init`).
- Bank comparison, dialogs and monthly overview (AP 3.5): `banking/domain/bank-comparison-logic.ts` (`sortBanks`, `nextBankSort`, `orderCost`, `BANK_COMPARISON_KEYS`; TS §7.3), `bankSort` in the banking slice and `selectBankComparison`; page `banking/feature/banks` with `banking/ui/bank-table` (conditions in words from `banking/ui/labels/bank-conditions-text.ts`, `aria-sort`, own scroll container). Dialogs `banking/feature/dialog-open-account`, `dialog-transfer` (`transferSchema` in `banking/ui/schemas`), `dialog-close-account` (shows `closingPreview`) and `dialog-salary-account`, opened by `BankingDialogEffects` on `clickedOpenAccount`/`clickedTransfer`/`clickedCloseAccount`/`clickedSalaryAccount` and mapped to the `submittedX` actions; actions per account through `banking/ui/account-actions` in the `[accountActions]` slots of `account-card` and `account-header`. The accounts page has the tabs „Konten“ and „Monatsübersicht“ (`banking/ui/monthly-overview`, `selectMonthlyOverview`), loaded by `loadMonthlyValues$` between the guards `enterAccountsPage`/`leaveAccountsPage` and refreshed throttled (`MONTHLY_VALUES_THROTTLE_MS`) on net-worth changes and month ends. The `select` helper in `src/testing/dom.ts` fires `input` and `change` like browsers.
- Reservations (AP 5.1b/c, TS §7.5, §8.4): `AccountView.reserved` (reservations of active buy orders; only open clearing accounts, else 0) next to `available` = max(0, available amount − reserved); fixtures that build an `AccountView` set `reserved: 0`.
- Custody fee and pending payouts (AP 5.2, TS §7.3, §7.4): `banking/domain/custody-fee-logic.ts` (`quarterMonths`, `daysInQuarter`, `averageDepotValue`, `quarterCustodyFee` with year share ¼ × days ÷ quarter days, `closingCustodyFees`, `openCustodyAccrual`, `accrueCustodyDay`, `closeCustodyQuarter`), `CustodyAccrual`/`CustodyFeeCharge` in `household.model.ts`, `startsQuarter` exported from `bank-run-logic.ts`, bank run step `custody-fee` after `account-fee` on the first bank working day of a quarter. `PlayerState.custody` per depot (`openDepot` adds it); `BankingSystem.accrueCustody(valueOf)` at each day change (end-of-day values from `Valuation`), `closeMonth` moves the quarter to pending, `runBank` books `custody-fee` (params `from`, `to`, `days`, `averageValue`), `settlement` of a clearing account adds the closing custody fees. `closingTargetError(…, pendingDistributions)` and `ClosingPreview.pendingDistributions`: a closing account with payouts still due needs a target; `BankCommands` redirects them (`DistributionSystem.redirect`); the close dialog asks for a target then.
- Taxes on sales and payouts (AP 5.3, TS §7.7, §8.3, §11.3; US-ST-01–04): `taxes/domain/tax-logic.ts`: `assessTaxableIncome(state, amount, kind)` with `TAXABLE_INCOME_KINDS` (`interest`, `distribution`, `sale`; only sales may be negative); `TaxYear` also holds `interest`, `distributions`, `gains`, `losses` (≤ 0), `paid`, `refunded` (`income` = their sum, `withheld` = `paid − refunded`); `lossCarryForwardOf`, `usedAllowance`, `taxYearSummary` (`TaxYearSummary` for the tax overview), `taxPreview(state, realizedGain)` and `saleTaxPreview(state, lots, quantity, proceeds)` (`SaleTaxPreview`: `costBasis`, `realizedGain`, `tax`, `net`). `BankingSystem.taxCapitalIncome(tick, accountId, income, kind, refs)` books `tax`/`tax-refund` against `external:tax-office` right after the triggering credit on its account (sales: `realizedGain`, refs `orderId`/`depotId`/`instrumentId`; payouts: gross amount, refs `depotId`/`instrumentId`) and returns the delta; `OrderSystem` and `DistributionSystem` call it through their ports. `closeYear` pushes the closed year to `PlayerState.closedTaxYears` (oldest first). Projection `projection.taxes` (`TaxesProjection` `{ current, closed }`, `engine/domain/tax-projection.ts`) with `full` and whenever `player.taxYear` is a new object; `EngineService.taxes$` (`TaxesProjectionUpdate` with `tick`, `full`). Slice `taxes` (`taxes/feature/state`, `provideTaxesState()` in `provideGameState()`, `TaxesFacade`: `currentTaxYear$`, `currentTaxSummary$`, `taxOverview$`, `saleTaxPreview$(lots, quantity, proceeds)`); `watchTaxes$` like `watchProjection$`; selectors `selectTaxOverview` (running year first, then closed newest first), `selectCurrentTaxSummary`, `selectSaleTaxPreview` (`null` without projection or for quantities the lots do not hold). Scenarios `engine/domain/game-engine-taxes.spec.ts` (broker without fees, test market without spread, accumulating ETF), tax invariants in `engine-properties.spec.ts`, host test `engine-host-taxes.spec.ts`. The taxes page is still a placeholder (AP 5.5).
